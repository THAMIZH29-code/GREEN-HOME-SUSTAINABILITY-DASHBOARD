Calculation Methodology
1. Electricity Tier-Slab Model

Units up to 100 kWh are calculated at 70% of base rate, 101-300 kWh at base rate, and 300+ kWh at 130% surcharge + Fixed Base Charge.

2. Water Benchmark Model

Calculated using Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) standard benchmark: $\text{Persons} \times 135 \text{ Liters/Day} \times 30 \text{ Days}$.

3. Model Accuracy Percentage

Formula: $100\% - \left( \frac{|\text{Actual Bill} - \text{Estimated Bill}|}{\text{Actual Bill}} \times 100 \right)$.
